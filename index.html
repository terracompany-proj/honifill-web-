Our Product Range

Engineered for Strength. Designed for Versatility.

We manufacture a complete range of paper honeycomb-based products designed to deliver exceptional strength, sustainability, and versatility. From cores and panels to pallets, boxes and packaging sleeves — our solutions are engineered to meet the evolving needs of modern industries with eco-friendly efficiency.
Honifill delivers a diverse portfolio of paper honeycomb solutions built to handle the toughest industrial demands while reducing environmental impact. Every product is a blend of precision engineering, durability and eco-conscious design.

Paper Honeycomb Core / Infill

We offer lightweight, high-strength honeycomb core solutions for structural applications such as metal door infill for added strength without extra weight, furniture infill for efficient material use in tables and cabinets, and event or exhibition structures that allow for easy setup and dismantling of stages, displays, and panels.

Paper Honeycomb Pallets

We offer eco-friendly alternatives to wood and plastic pallets. Our lightweight, recyclable paper-based pallets are ideal for shipping and logistics across FMCG, pharma, electronics, retail, and exports, delivering a cost-effective and sustainable solution.

Paper Honeycomb Boards / Panels

Our multi-purpose structural and protective boards are ideal for partitions, wall panels, carton separators, warehouse racking, edge protection, and safe packaging of glass, doors, and mirrors. Durable and shock-absorbing, they ensure reliable protection during storage and transit.

Honeycomb Packaging

We offer eco-friendly alternatives to wood and plastic pallets. Our lightweight, recyclable paper-based pallets are ideal for shipping and logistics across FMCG, pharmaceuticals, electronics, retail, and exports, providing a cost-effective and sustainable solution.

Honeycomb Boxes

High-strength and lightweight honeycomb boxes designed for safe packing of heavy, fragile, and export-bound products. They offer excellent load-bearing capacity, impact resistance, and superior protection while being eco-friendly and fully recyclable.

Honey Sleves & Inserts

Reinforced protective components designed for palletized and industrial packaging. They provide strong edge support, impact resistance, and load stability, ensuring safe handling and damage-free transportation.

Pads & Blocks

Honeycomb pads and blocks are designed to provide superior cushioning and load-bearing support for heavy and fragile products during storage and transportation.

Crates & Pallet Boxes

Honeycomb crates and pallet boxes offer a durable and eco-friendly alternative to wooden crates and plastic containers used in industrial logistics.

Edge Protectors Corner Boards

Honeycomb edge protectors and corner boards are essential packaging components designed to protect the edges and corners of products during handling, stacking, and transportation
