Edge Protectors & Corner Boards

Honeycomb edge protectors and corner boards are essential packaging components designed to protect the edges and corners of products during handling, stacking, and transportation.

These protective boards strengthen palletized loads, prevent strap damage, and ensure that cartons and packages maintain their shape under pressure. Their rigid construction helps distribute weight evenly and reduces the risk of product damage caused by compression or impact.

Reinforced Protection for Palletized & Fragile Goods

Honifill’s paper-based edge protectors offer high rigidity and corner stability for palletized loads. They minimize transit damage, improve stacking efficiency, and offer a 100% recyclable alternative to plastic and foam protectors.
